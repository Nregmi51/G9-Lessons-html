Health is Wealth

Once upon a time, there lived a benevolentदयालु — kind and generous and kind-heartedदयालु हृदयको — having a kind nature king. But, the people weren't happy with him because he was very lazy and would not do anything other than eating and sleeping.

He spent days, weeks and months in his bed either eating something or sleeping. The king became a couch potatoसोफामा बसेर धेरै समय बिताउने अल्छी मानिस — a lazy person who spends time sitting/watching TV and the people started to worry about him.

One day, the king realised that he couldn't move his body, not even his foot. He became very large and his obesityमोटोपन — the condition of being extremely fat became a matter of fun, calling him 'bulkyठूलो र गह्रौं — very large and heavy king'.

The king invited expert doctors from various parts of his country and offered them generous rewardsउदार पुरस्कारहरू — large gifts given willingly to make him fit. Unfortunately, none could help him gain his health and fitness back. The king spent an enormousधेरै ठूलो — very large amount of money but everything went in vainव्यर्थमा — without success.

One fine morning, a holy man visited the country. He heard about the ill-health of the king, and informed the minister at the palace that he could easily cure the king. Hearing these promising words, the minister became very happy. He requested the king to meet the holy man to get rid of his problem.

The holy man residedबसोबास गर्नु — lived (in a place) at a distant place. Since the king could not move his body, he asked the minister to bring the holy man to the palace, but the holy man refusedअस्वीकार गर्नु — said no to something. He said that the king had to go to him, in order to get curedनिको हुनु — made well again.

After strenuousमिहिनेत चाहिने, कठिन — requiring great effort efforts, the king met the holy man at the latter's residence. The holy man complimented the king saying that he was a good ruler, and said that he would soon regain his health. He asked the king to come for treatment the next day. He also told the king would be treated only if he came on foot to the holy man's residence.

The king was unable to walk even a few steps on the road, but aidedमद्दत गरिएको — helped by his followers, he reached the holy man's place. Unfortunately, the holy man was not available there and his devoteeभक्त — a person devoted to someone requested the king to come and meet him the next day for treatment.

This was repeated for two weeks and the king never met the holy man, and never had any treatment.

Graduallyबिस्तारै — slowly, over time, the king realised that he felt a lot lighter, lost a considerableउल्लेखनीय, धेरै — fairly large in amount amount of weight and felt more active than before. He realised the reason why the holy man asked him to reach his place by walking.

Very soon, the king regainedपुनः प्राप्त गर्नु — got back something lost his health, and the people were very happy in his kingdom.

B. Answer the following questions

a. Why did people not like the king though he was kind?
Although the king was kind-hearted, people didn't like him because he was very lazy and did nothing except eat and sleep all day.
b. Why were the specialists called to the palace and offered huge prizes?
The king invited expert doctors and offered them generous rewards to help him regain his health and fitness, as he had become too obese to move.
c. Why did the minister become so happy?
The minister became happy because a holy man had informed him that he could easily cure the king's ill-health.
d. Why did the holy man tell the king to come to his place on foot for treatment?
The holy man wanted the king to walk to his place daily so that the king would unknowingly do physical exercise and lose weight, which was the real cure.
e. Why did the holy man disappear after the first meeting with the king?
The holy man deliberately stayed away so the king would have to keep walking to his residence every day for two weeks, helping him lose weight through exercise.
f. How did the king recover his health?
The king recovered his health by walking daily to the holy man's residence for two weeks, which made him lighter, more active, and healthier — even though he never actually received any "treatment."
g. What lesson did you learn from the story?
The lesson is that regular physical activity/exercise (like walking) is essential for good health, and true wealth lies in good health ("Health is Wealth").

Short Questions

1. What kind of person was the king at the beginning of the story?
He was a benevolent but very lazy king who only ate and slept.
2. Who visited the country and claimed he could cure the king?
A holy man visited the country and claimed he could cure the king.
3. What condition did the holy man put before treating the king?
The holy man said he would treat the king only if the king came to his residence on foot.
4. For how long did the king keep visiting the holy man without receiving treatment?
The king kept visiting for two weeks without receiving any treatment.
5. What was the real secret behind the king's recovery?
The real secret was the daily walking exercise, not any medicine or magical treatment.
